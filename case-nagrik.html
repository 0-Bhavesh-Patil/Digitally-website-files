The Challenge

Accessibility cannot be an afterthought.

Most public municipal web portals suffer from dense text walls, low-contrast color choices, tiny touch targets, and complex form structures.

This created severe barriers for senior citizens and non-English speaking residents attempting to submit municipal grievances, track sanitation requests, or pay civic utility bills online.

Inclusive design in action.

Inclusive Approach

Designing for universal access.

01 / Inclusive Audit

Accessibility Mapping

Tested UI elements with screen readers (NVDA, TalkBack) and high-contrast color checkers to guarantee WCAG 2.1 AA compliance.

02 / Vernacular First

Native Multilingual UX

Designed seamless instant language switching (Marathi, Hindi, English) with culturally contextual icons and clear typography.

03 / Touch Targets

48px+ Ergonomic Targets

Enlarged all interactive buttons and inputs to 48px+ touch targets for effortless tap accuracy on any device screen size.

04 / Voice Assistance

Guided Audio Feedback

Integrated voice-prompt support for visual guidance during form submissions and ticket tracking status check-ins.
