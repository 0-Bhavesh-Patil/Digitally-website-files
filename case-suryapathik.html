The Challenge

A critical safety question, tackled at pace under real hackathon pressure.

Track maintenance and obstacle detection on high-speed rail corridors historically relied on periodic physical trackman patrols, creating dangerous blind spots between patrol intervals.

The challenge was to design and prototype a real-time monitoring solution capable of detecting millimeter-scale track shifts, thermal expansion stress, and physical track obstacles—and instantly alerting oncoming locomotive drivers before entering the risk zone.

System Design

Connected hardware & high-clarity software.

01 / IoT Sensor Nodes

Track Vibration & Stress

Deploys low-power acoustic vibration and thermal sensors along track sections to detect structural micro-fractures in real time.

02 / Edge Telemetry

Real-Time AI Processing

Runs lightweight anomaly classification models on edge microcontrollers to filter false positives before transmitting alerts.

03 / Driver Cab Interface

High-Contrast HUD

Designed a high-urgency driver cockpit display giving clear visual and auditory distance-to-hazard indicators.

04 / Central Control

Control Room Telemetry

Provides central railway controllers with a live map overview of track health, maintenance schedules, and automated incident logs.
